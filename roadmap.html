Stage 01 Complete

Identity and Philosophy

Cabin now has a name, domain, GitHub organisation, logo, typographic voice and restrained colour system. Its first product principles establish how the application should grow without overwhelming the writer.

Stage 02 Current

Personas and Research

Study first-time novelists, worldbuilders, screenwriters, researchers and students in their real writing environments. Their habits, frustrations and existing tools will determine which problems Cabin should solve first.

Stage 03 Beyond the mist

MVP Selection

Reduce the broad feature inventory to a focused first release that protects the core writing experience. Every deferred capability receives an intentional later place instead of quietly expanding the initial scope.

Stage 04 Future

UX and File Model

Define how layouts, panels, resizing, docking and alternate views behave before implementation begins. At the same time, design a durable project folder structure that remains legible outside Cabin.

Stage 05 Future

Design System

Build the shared spacing, typography, icons, menus, sidebars, modals and theme tokens behind every screen. The system must preserve Cabin’s calm character from the smallest dialog to the densest workspace.

Stage 06 Future

Prototype

Create a pure interface prototype for testing resizing, docking, workspace layouts and motion without persistence getting in the way. The prototype should expose awkward interactions while they are still inexpensive to change.

Stage 07 Future

Core Editor

Implement projects, documents, the editor, autosave, themes and tabs with reliability as the baseline. Writing, saving and reopening work must feel uneventful before Cabin earns more advanced capabilities.

Stage 08 Future

Project System

Add folders, chapters, scenes, drag-and-drop ordering, metadata and workspace presets around the stable editor. Search and dependable exports will make larger manuscripts navigable without hiding their underlying files.

Stage 09 Future

Closed Alpha

Invite a small, varied group of writers and observe how they use Cabin without over-explaining it. Their sessions will reveal confusing assumptions, fragile workflows and the rough edges that internal testing misses.

Stage 10 Future

Beta

Open Cabin to a broader group of writers working on real, long-running projects. Improve reliability, measure recurring friction and confirm that the calm workflow survives greater scale and variety.

Stage 11 Future

Deployment

Prepare signed public builds, clear documentation, predictable update flows and thoroughly tested exports. Writers should understand where their work lives and trust Cabin throughout an ongoing project.

Stage 12 Future

Community Growth

Grow a thoughtful community around writing practice, plugins, themes and transparent product notes. Expansion should deepen the craft around Cabin without turning the core application into a marketplace or social feed.
