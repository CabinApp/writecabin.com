A route through the mountains

Twelve places.
One careful path.

Stage 01 Complete

Identity and philosophy

Name, domain, GitHub organisation, logo, typography, colour palette and first product philosophy are in place.

Stage 02 Current

Personas and research

Understand first-time novelists, worldbuilders, screenwriters, researchers and students before choosing the first feature set.

Stage 03 Beyond the mist

MVP selection

Turn the large feature inventory into a restrained first version, placing everything else deliberately into later work.

Stage 04 Future

UX and file model

Sketch layouts, panels, resizing, docking, views and project folder structure before implementation begins.

Stage 05 Future

Design system

Build spacing, typography, icons, menus, sidebars, modals and themes so the interface feels calm at every scale.

Stage 06 Future

Prototype

Create a pure interface prototype to test resizing, docking, layouts and motion before connecting persistence.

Stage 07 Future

Core editor

Implement projects, documents, editor, autosave, themes and tabs—with reliability as the baseline.

Stage 08 Future

Project system

Add folders, chapters, scenes, drag-and-drop ordering, metadata, workspace presets, search and exports.

Stage 09 Future

Closed alpha

Recruit a small group of writers, observe without over-explaining, take notes and repair rough edges.

Stage 10 Future

Beta

Open Cabin to a broader group of writers, improve reliability and test whether the calm workflow holds under real projects.

Stage 11 Future

Deployment

Prepare public builds, documentation, update flows and export confidence so writers can trust Cabin with ongoing work.

Stage 12 Future

Community growth

Grow a thoughtful community around writing practice, plugins, themes and transparent product notes.
