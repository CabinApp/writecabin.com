Chapter one

The house at low tide

By the time Mara reached the house, the sea had given back the path.

She stood at the gate with her father’s key in her palm. Beyond it, the windows held the last of the afternoon light, and somewhere inside, a clock was still keeping time.

Nothing had changed. That was the strange part. The blue door, the crooked letterbox, the rosemary growing through the step. All of it had carried on without her.

She turned the key.

For the first draft

Keep a single manuscript, or build a tree of chapters and scenes. Rearrange the story as it takes shape.

For your own rhythm

Choose Dawn or Aurora. Adjust your manuscript’s typeface and line spacing. Show, hide or resize the navigation.

For a bigger world Planned

Dedicated research, notes and character tools are part of Cabin’s direction. More space for your world, when those tools are ready.

Your words.
Yours to keep.

A story can take years. Your access to it shouldn’t depend on a connection.

Cabin stores projects in a folder on your device. Write offline. Keep your files. The manuscript lives independently of the app.

Cloud features, if added, will be optional. Your local writing comes first.
