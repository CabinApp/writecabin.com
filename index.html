Chapter one

The first morning
arrived quietly.

The mountain kept its secrets beneath a white fold of weather. Elian waited by the cold stove, listening for the bell that only rang when someone crossed the old bridge.

When it came, the sound was small and silver. Not a warning. Not yet.

DESIGN STUDY / 01—03
01 / Space

Quiet by default.

Chapters, research and timelines have a place. They enter only when the manuscript needs them.

02 / Ownership

Your words. Your files.

Local folders, readable Markdown and open exports. Your writing should remain yours when the app is closed.

03 / Voice

A human at the centre.

AI may help you question a sentence. It never writes it for you. The voice on the page stays human.

03 / The light is on

Built slowly.
With intention.

Cabin is in product design. I’m working out its workflows, file model and interface before development begins. You can follow along as the room takes shape.
