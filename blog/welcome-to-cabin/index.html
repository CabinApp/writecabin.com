Welcome to Cabin

Cabin is writing software designed to grow with you.

1 min read

Hi! Welcome to Cabin!

Cabin is writing software that is designed to grow with you. Arising from my personal frustration with existing writing programs always missing one feature or another, I decided to use my software development knowledge to create an app that grows with you.

The Purpose of this Blog

I have only recently conceptualised and started work on Cabin. This blog will be a running collection of insights, design choices, research findings, and devlogs. You can gain an insight into my process and track the progress of the project on a much finer scale than the Roadmap
