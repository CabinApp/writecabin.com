I spent a lot of time listing down features that could go in Cabin. Every time I thought "That's it, this is all a writer could want", I found an obscure feature somewhere that surprisingly many people use and want?

## The Risk of Explosion

When defining the features for a project as large as Cabin, an explosion of features is always a risk. You think of one feature, and five more join the party as "related features". Hence, I had to be really careful while deciding the features for v1 of Cabin, because if I decided on too many features, I would grow a white beard by the time I was done.

## The Explosion Happened

Yeah so I kinda made a big list. In retrospect, it isn't very long as most of the information is redundant or "types" of notes, but there was some complicated stuff stated in just a few lines (such as a full plugin ecosystem and API).

Somewhere along the process of identifying features, I realised that there is no way I can implement this in a feasible period of time. So I created a new list. This time, keeping only stuff that was essential.

## What Even is a Minimum Lovable Cabin?

In terms of creating the first version of a product, there are two concepts: MVPs and MLPs.
I usually define them as

- MVPs (Minimum Viable Products) are a bare-bones version of the product that just show its core functionality.
- MLPs (Minimum Lovable Products), on the other hand, are a version of the product wherein someone could actually USE it as a complete product.

My goal was to make an MLP for Cabin, which I have decided to call a Minimum Lovable Cabin (MLC). This meant removing all the features that were not required for an individual user. This also led to a precarious balancing situation. If I included fewer features, it would not be Cabin, it would just be another text editor. If I added too many... well we know how that would turn out.

## What I Decided

In my MLC feature list, I decided to remove all things that relied on the _community_ aspect of building Cabin. Plugins, user-made themes, etc. The next thing I removed was AI. I know there are people out there who use AI for writing assistance, and it is in my mind as a feature to add to Cabin. But personally, I don't feel the need for AI in the first version of Cabin.

Anyways, I won't burn your eyes with the entire feature list, but just know that I kept the features that were crucial to Cabin's identity and kept a few in a tentative state (such as in-built mood music).

## A Clarification About Rejection

A feature that did not make it into the MLC list, such as custom themes, has not been incinerated. It's just something I will let future me worry about. The aim of identifying MLC features was to show what makes Cabin **different**.
